## Clearance-sensitive feasible-candidate reranking

- Split avoidance feasibility from ranking cost. `max_violation` still controls
  `satisfied()`, while `AvoidRegion` and `AvoidProjection` now add a positive rational
  soft-clearance cost that decreases with minimum signed distance.
- Added `clearance_scale` to constraint configs and JSON. Evaluation applies the
  configured value to generated or precomputed constraints, nominal-path generation
  serializes it, and the default is 5 cm. `--avoid-clearance-scale 0` restores the
  historical hinge-only score.
- Kept rejection's first-feasible behavior and ITPS energy unchanged. Reranking now
  has a non-zero obstacle-distance signal when every sampled candidate is feasible.
